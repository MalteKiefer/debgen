# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the DebGen community to be welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment. We commit to making participation productive for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and good-faith questions.
- Give and accept feedback about ideas and work without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not share private information without permission.
- Focus on evidence and safety when discussing repositories, packages, and trust data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or unsafe.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or other interactions that do not follow it. Responses may range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/MalteKiefer). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed fairly.
